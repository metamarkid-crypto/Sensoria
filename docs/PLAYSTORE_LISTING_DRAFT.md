Sensoria — Every Voice Truly Matters 💙

Sensoria is an AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) app that helps children with speech delay, autism, or other special needs express themselves independently — and connects them directly with mom and dad.

🎨 CHILD MODE — LEARN & EXPRESS
• 244 pictogram words with clear, child-friendly images

• Closed communication — only between linked child and parent devices
