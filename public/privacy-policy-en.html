Privacy Policy

We design Sensoria for children — and that starts with respecting their privacy. This page explains what data we process, why, and how you stay in control.

Summary

Sensoria is an Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) app for children. The experience is split into two roles: Child Mode for communication and Companion (parent) Mode for setup and monitoring. All app data is stored in Supabase (backend provider, headquartered in Ireland/EEA). We do not show ads and we never sell data.

🛡️
Core principles

We collect as little data as possible, process it only for communication and family coordination, keep it accessible only to the paired family, and delete it on request.

Not collected: full name, address, photos of the child, biometric data, advertising data, contacts, installed app lists, browsing history. The advertising identifier (GAID/IDFA) is never read by the app.

Communication content is never read by humans, never used to train models, and never shared outside the paired family.

How We Use Data

  • Running the core app: card grid, sentence building, text-to-speech, favorites, custom cards.
  • Delivering the child's AAC messages to the paired companion device and keeping family chat history.
  • Showing companion replies to the child, either as speech or as illustrated visual replies ("Okay / No / Wait") when enabled.
  • Showing the child's location on the companion dashboard when the companion enables the feature (off by default).
  • Displaying the device's last-active time ("last seen").
  • Managing device quotas and subscriptions when you subscribe.
  • Preventing abuse and keeping the service secure.

We do not use data for cross-app behavioral profiling, targeted advertising, or data sales.

Data Sharing

We only share data with the following providers, each bound by a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) — database, authentication, realtime, storage; running on AWS infrastructure with Southeast Asia region availability.
  • Google Play — app distribution & subscription billing (when applicable).

We never share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party beyond the list above. A child's communication data is shared with no one except paired family devices.

International transfers (when they occur) rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

Your Rights

  • Access & copies — request a copy of your child's data via the contact below.
  • Rectification — edit the profile directly in Companion Mode → Profile.
  • Erasure — unlink the device in Companion Mode → Devices, or contact us.
  • Withdraw consent — turn location off at any time from the companion dashboard.
  • Portability — profile & message data can be exported (JSON) on request.
  • You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy & Families Policy Compliance

Sensoria is designed for children and follows Google's Families Policy and COPPA:

  • No ads, no mature content, nothing that solicits personal data directly from children.
  • Child data is created only by a companion in Companion Mode — verifiable parental/guardian consent.
  • No open social features — communication happens only with paired family members (closed system).
  • Children are never asked for unnecessary identity details (nickname only).
  • Location tracking is off by default and consciously enabled by the companion.
  • At any time, the companion can delete the child's data (unlink / delete profile).

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS; data is stored in Supabase protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — each family can only read its own data. Administrative access is restricted, audited, and protected by strong authentication.

Location Data

📍
Off by default — enabled only by the companion

Location tracking is not active out of the box. When a companion enables it, the child's position appears on the companion dashboard to support the child's safety. Coordinates are shared with no one except paired family devices, deleted automatically after 30 days, and can be turned off at any time.

What we collect when enabled

  • Latitude/longitude of the child device, timestamp, optional place label.

What we NEVER do

  • Sell or share coordinates with third parties.
  • Link location to advertising or ad profiles.
  • Use location for anything other than the family safety map.

How to control it

Turn it off from the companion dashboard at any time — disabling stops all collection. Uninstalling the app also stops all collection. Contact us to delete location history earlier.

Policy Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
