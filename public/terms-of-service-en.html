Summary

By downloading, installing, or using the Sensoria AAC app (the "App"), you agree to these Terms of Service. Sensoria is an AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) app consisting of Child Mode (communication) and Companion Mode (setup & monitoring). If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

👨‍👩‍👧
Who agrees

These Terms are accepted by the parent/guardian companion — not by the child. Children use the App under their companion's supervision and responsibility.

Definitions

Child ModeThe AAC communication interface for children: card grid, sentence building, speech, and favorites.
Companion ModeThe interface for parents/guardians: child profile, settings, device pairing, and monitoring.
Child DeviceA device where Child Mode is active and used by the child.
Companion DeviceA parent/guardian device paired with a Child Device.
PairingLinking a Companion Device to a Child Device so messages and family data can sync.
PremiumThe App's paid features (e.g. premium voices, location tracking, extra device slots) unlocked through paid plans.
PlanA time-based subscription scheme (e.g. 1/3/6/12 months) defining the active Premium period.

Accounts & Devices

  • The App does not require a traditional account; a device identity is created automatically at device registration.
  • Pairing may only be performed by a parent/guardian using the code/QR generated by the Child Device.
  • You are responsible for keeping pairing codes/QR confidential. If a device is lost or stolen, unlink it immediately from Companion Mode.
  • The companion device quota per child follows your active Plan; additional devices can be added through available slots.

Use by Children

Sensoria is designed for children with companion supervision. Specific terms:

  • Parental consent — the child profile may only be created and edited by a parent/guardian in Companion Mode.
  • Closed communication system — children can only message paired Companion Devices; there are no open social features, no conversations with strangers, and the send button locks automatically when no companion is connected.
  • No transactions by children — children cannot make any purchases; all purchases and settings happen only in Companion Mode.
  • Full companion control — parents/guardians control settings (voice, appearance, accessibility, location) and can enable/disable features at any time, including remotely.
  • Data minimization — we only ask for what is needed (nickname, gender, age). Details are in the Privacy Policy.
  • Supervision still matters — Sensoria supports communication but does not replace adult supervision.
⚠️
Prohibited

Allowing anyone other than a parent/guardian to pair with a child device, using a child's data beyond family communication purposes, or leaving an inaccurate child profile in place.

Premium & Subscriptions

  • Free trial — a new Child Device may receive a Premium trial of the length displayed in the app (if available). The trial applies once per child device.
  • Subscription period — Plans run for the chosen duration (e.g. 1/3/6/12 months) from verified payment. Remaining days from previous purchases are added to the new period (not forfeited).
  • Combo scope — one subscription covers 1 Child Device + its paired companion devices, per the rules displayed in the app.
  • No auto-renewal — subscriptions do not auto-renew. When the period ends, Premium stops and you may renew manually at any time; family data is not deleted and free features keep working.
  • Device slots — additional companion slot purchases extend capacity and follow the lifetime of the related subscription.
  • Price changes — price changes apply only to future purchases and never affect a period already paid for.
  • Cancellation by you — simply let the period end without renewing; or contact us to deactivate early.

Payments

  • Payment channels — purchases are processed through the channels available and displayed in the app, depending on availability in your region.
  • Verification — purchases are verified once funds are received; status can be checked on the purchase screen ("I Have Paid — Verify"). Keep your proof of payment/order ID.
  • Pricing — prices are shown in Indonesian Rupiah and include applicable taxes as displayed on the purchase screen.
  • Payment data — we never store your card, bank account, or e-wallet details; transactions are processed by the relevant payment provider. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Failed transactions — if a verified payment does not activate Premium within 24 hours, contact us with proof of payment; we will activate manually or refund per the Refunds section.

Refunds

  • All purchases — request a refund via our contact within 24×3 hours of verified payment if the feature cannot be used due to our fault. Requests after Premium has been active and functioning normally are generally non-refundable.
  • Distribution channels — the store or channel where you obtained the app may apply its own refund policy, which also applies to you.
  • Service failure — if Premium cannot be enjoyed during an active period due to our fault and we fail to fix it within a reasonable time, we refund proportionally for the remaining period.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Using the App to harass, intimidate, or communicate with a child outside their legitimate family.
  • Pairing with a child device without parental/guardian authorization.
  • Misusing a child's location data for anything other than the child's safety.
  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, or creating derivative works beyond what the law allows.
  • Accessing, altering, or distributing another family's data.
  • Using the App for unlawful activity.
